The Patriots are currently 5.5-point favorites against the Steelers, according to odds posted at DraftKings Sportsbook.

🏈 T.J. Watt tied Pro Football Hall of Famers Chris Doleman, Bruce Smith and Lawrence Taylor for being named Defensive Player of the Week nine times in his career.

🏈 NFL Films posted the Darnell Washington stiff arm angle that Steelers fans have been waiting for.

🏈 Rob Gronkowski thinks if the Patriots lose to the Steelers in Week 2 that their playoff hopes could be gone.

🏈 Patriots All-Pro cornerback Christian Gonzalez talked about Aaron Rodgers and the Steelers offense.

🏈 Aaron Rodgers thinks the offenses stats in Week 1 were misconceiving.

“I mean, the whole thing can be deceiving. We had the PI on third down that could have been the touchdown. We had a PI on DK that wasn’t called,” Rodgers said. “We had an opportunity to [Roman Wilson] down the field that was not called. He didn’t come up with it. Had DK in the end zone, for sure PI, and he still almost made a ridiculous catch, and the one that DK obviously usually catches. One or two of those goes, and it’s a whole different conversation. That’s the way the league goes. You can’t get caught up in too many specific numbers without looking at what happened in the game.”

🏈 Former Steelers free safety and current New York Jet Minkah Fitzpatrick suffered a groin injury during Sunday’s win over the Tennessee Titans. He is expected to miss 2–3 weeks, according to NFL insider Jordan Schultz. Fitzpatrick played just six snaps on Sunday.

🏈 Former NFL defensive lineman Chris Canty doesn’t feel good about the Steelers after Week 1.

“You needed the pick six to seal a win against a team whoes quarterback got there late to camp, at the end of July. And then, oh by the way, we still don’t have an end to the Joey Porter contract negotiation saga. Have you seen the Baltimore Ravens and the Cincinnati Bengals? Have you seen what those teams look like? I don’t know how you can feel better about the Pittsburgh Steelers after Week 1,” Canty said on ESPN’s First Take.

🏈 Julian Edelman thought the Steelers offense looked its best in a long time.

“I thought the Steelers’ offense was the best it looked in a while. It looked different. Mike McCarthy has never struggled on offense. Yes, they’ve gotta tighten it up. But they were spreading them out, they had a lot of crossers,” Edelman said on NFL on FOX’s YouTube channel. “Last year, I felt like all I saw was four verticals. Every important thing was four verticals and hit big Washington, the tight end.”

🏈 Ex-NFL quarterback Kurt Benkert did a film breakdown on his former teammate Aaron Rodgers’ performance against the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday.

Benkert thinks it was a vintage outing by the four-time NFL MVP.
