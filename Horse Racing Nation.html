NTRA poll: Woodbine Mile repeat lifts Notable Speech into top 10

HRN staff September 15, 2026 9:37am
Photo: Rebecca Gullett / Eclipse Sportswire
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 3:33
Instaread

Notable Speech climbed 10 spots to no. 8 in the National Thoroughbred Racing Association's top Thoroughbred poll after his repeat victory in the Woodbine Mile (G1), and Sovereignty kept his grip on no. 1 in the week 34 rankings released Monday.

The 2025 Eclipse Award-winning turf male ran down pacesetting Deterministic inside the sixteenth pole to take the Woodbine Mile by 1 1/4 lengths Saturday. William Buick rode the 5-year-old son of Dubawi for trainer Charles Appleby. The win, his third in five starts this year, earned Notable Speech a fees-paid berth in the Breeders' Cup Mile on Oct. 31 at Keeneland, where he will defend his title. He collected 101 points after ranking 18th a week earlier.

Deterministic, whose six-race win streak ended in the Mile, slid from no. 6 to no. 9 with 100 points.

Sovereignty drew 23 of the 32 first-place votes and 297 points to lead for another week. Knightsbridge held no. 2 with three first-place votes and 264 points, and Nitrogen stayed third with 216.

Forever Young and Magnitude traded places. The Japan-bred horse picked up two first-place votes and moved to no. 4 with 178 points, one more than Magnitude, who received four first-place votes and slipped to no. 5 with 177.

The 3-year-olds Englishman and Golden Tempo each advanced one spot, to no. 6 with 119 points and no. 7 with 105, respectively.

Splendora took sole possession of no. 10 with 69 points after a 5 1/4-length romp Saturday in the Locust Grove Stakes (G2) at Churchill Downs. The Bob Baffert-trained mare, the 2025 champion female sprinter, improved to 5-for-6 this year.

Leading Change and Burnham Square dropped out of the top 10. Leading Change topped the others receiving votes with 34 points, and Burnham Square followed with 22.
