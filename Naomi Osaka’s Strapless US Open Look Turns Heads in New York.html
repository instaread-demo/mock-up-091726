Naomi Osaka’s Strapless US Open Look Turns Heads in New York

Naomi Osaka has given fans an early glimpse of her 2026 US Open style, and the first look has already sparked plenty of reactions. The Japanese tennis star shared photos from Arthur Ashe Stadium showing off a strapless orange outfit before practice.

With floral details, heels, and a rosette-covered bag, the outfit quickly became a talking point before the tournament had even started.

The 28-year-old has built a reputation for bringing personal style into major tennis events. Her outfits often carry a creative theme, making her appearances off the court almost as closely watched as her matches.

Earlier in 2026, Osaka wore a jellyfish-inspired outfit at the Australian Open before her first-round match against Antonia Ruzic. Her latest look takes a different direction but keeps the same attention to detail.

Osaka posted photos of the outfit on X ahead of the US Open. The pictures showed her inside an empty Arthur Ashe Stadium, where she posed in a strapless miniskirt-and-top combination made from orange fabric.

The outfit featured floral embellishments around the bustline and along the sides. She completed the look with a shoulder bag covered in rosettes, brown heels, and her tennis racquets.

Fans React to the Outfit

Instagram | @protrending | Naomi Osaka continues to bring bold fashion to tennis, from her latest look to her eye catching jellyfish inspired outfit at the Australian Open.

The response arrived quickly, with fans focusing on Osaka’s fashion choice before her first competitive appearance at the tournament.

Taylor Townsend, another professional tennis player, joined the reactions with a simple comment: “So cuteeee.”

Other fans praised Osaka with playful references to her tennis skills and fashion sense. One wrote, “Already serving before the tournament has even begun,” while another said, “Serving on and off the court.”

A third fan called her “The people’s princess” and described the outfit as adorable. Another commented, “Naomi, are you wearing that dress or is it wearing you, absolute fire.”

The reactions reflect how much interest surrounds Osaka’s tournament outfits, especially when she introduces a new look before a major event.

Osaka has previously spoken about how much she enjoys discussing clothing with fans. During Wimbledon in 2026, she explained that fashion sometimes feels easier for her to discuss than tennis.

“I feel like I’m a lot more equipped to talk about my clothes than to talk about my tennis,” Osaka said.

She added, “It’s strange because I’ve been playing tennis for 20 something years but some days I don’t feel like an expert on it.”

Osaka also said she could keep talking about an outfit for a long time and enjoys seeing people examine its fabric, construction, and story.

US Open Title Chase

Osaka enters the 2026 US Open seeking her third championship at Flushing Meadows and the fourth Grand Slam singles title of her career.

She won her first US Open in 2018 after defeating Serena Williams in the final. Two years later, she claimed the title again by beating Victoria Azarenka.

The qualifying rounds concluded before the main draw took place on Sunday, August 30. The tournament began on September 2, putting Osaka’s first competitive appearance close behind her much-discussed practice look.

Naomi Osaka’s latest outfit has created early buzz around her 2026 US Open appearance, but the attention will soon shift back to the court. With two previous US Open titles and another Grand Slam goal in sight, her tournament will offer plenty to watch.

For now, her orange strapless look has given fans an early preview of the style she brings to New York.
