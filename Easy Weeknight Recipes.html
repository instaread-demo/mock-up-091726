My Kids Devour This Squash and Zucchini Casserole!

We all know that some kids will happily munch on just about any vegetable, while others can be a little more hesitant when it comes to anything green… or squash-like. Can I get a witness?

But this delicious squash and zucchini casserole might just win them over! It’s layered with melted cheese, a creamy sauce, tender zucchini and yellow squash, and a crispy Panko topping. Even picky eaters will have a hard time saying no!

Recipe Ingredients

  • Fresh Zucchini and Yellow Squash: Slice into ½-inch-thick rounds, or spiralize if you prefer.
  • Butter: Use 1 stick of unsalted butter, softened to room temperature.
  • Flour: All-purpose flour thickens the creamy sauce.
  • Milk: I use regular milk, but lower-fat milk works, too.
  • Italian seasoning: Use your favorite Italian seasoning blend, or swap in another herb and spice blend you love.
  • Fresh Garlic: Mince or press the garlic before adding it to the sauce.
  • Olive Oil: For sautéing the zucchini and squash and drizzling over the casserole. Avocado oil or another neutral cooking oil will also work.
  • Salt and Pepper: Season to taste.
  • Mozzarella and Parmesan: I use shredded mozzarella and grated Parmesan for the cheesy layers, but you can swap in your favorite shredded cheese.
  • Panko: Regular or gluten-free Panko breadcrumbs will work.

How to Make Zucchini and Squash Casserole

  1. Prep: Preheat the oven to 375˚F. Grease a 9×13-inch baking dish with butter and set aside. Cut the zucchini and yellow squash into ½-inch thick slices. Place the sliced vegetables in a colander, sprinkle with salt, and let them stand to drain.
  2. Make the cream sauce: Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 2 minutes, whisking constantly. Slowly add the milk and Italian seasoning, whisking constantly until smooth. Simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  3. Dry and season the vegetables: Use paper towels to pat the zucchini and squash completely dry. Transfer them to a large bowl and toss with the garlic, a drizzle of olive oil, and a little salt and pepper.
  4. Sauté the veggies: Heat the remaining tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the vegetables and cook for about 3 minutes, stirring frequently, until they just begin to soften. Remove from the heat, drain any excess juices, and set aside.
  5. Layer the casserole: Spoon ¾ cup of the cream sauce over the bottom of the prepared baking dish. Arrange half of the sautéed zucchini and squash over the sauce, then sprinkle with 1 cup of mozzarella and ⅓ cup of Parmesan. Repeat the layers with the remaining vegetables, mozzarella, and Parmesan. Sprinkle the Panko over the top and drizzle with a little olive oil.
  6. Bake: Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the casserole is bubbling around the edges. Remove from the oven and let cool for 10 minutes before serving with a garnish of fresh parsley and grated Parmesan.

Recipe Tips

  • Don’t overcook the squash: Sauté the squash and zucchini just until they begin to soften. They’ll continue cooking in the oven, so overcooking them in the skillet can make the casserole mushier.
  • Taste and adjust the seasoning: The vegetables, cream sauce, and cheeses all add flavor to the casserole, so season each component properly. Taste the sauce and vegetables before assembling the casserole, and adjust the salt and pepper as needed.
  • Squash options: If you don’t have both yellow squash and zucchini on hand, you can make this casserole with just one or the other.

Easy Variations

  • Try different seasonings: Swap the Italian seasoning for Cajun seasoning for a little kick, or try lemon pepper, seafood seasoning, herbes de Provence, or your favorite garlic and herb blend.
  • Vegetable options: Use this same basic recipe to make a broccoli and cauliflower casserole. Simply replace the squash with lightly steamed broccoli and cauliflower.
  • Make it a main dish: Enjoy this zucchini casserole as a vegetarian dinner, or make it heartier by adding layers of cooked Italian sausage, chicken, or diced ham.

What To Serve with Zucchini Squash Casserole

  • Seafood: Pair it with Old Bay roasted salmon for an easy dinner. The bold seasoning is great with the creamy, cheesy casserole.
  • Chicken: These pan-seared Cajun butter chicken thighs are juicy, flavorful, and go with just about anything.
  • Dessert: Finish dinner with Southern banana pudding, made with layers of creamy vanilla pudding, bananas, and fluffy meringue.

Zucchini and Squash Casserole

by Katerina | Easy Weeknight Recipes
Easy zucchini and squash casserole made with tender yellow squash and zucchini, baked in a rich cream sauce with plenty of cheese. This creamy, cheesy squash casserole is a comforting vegetable side dish for family dinners, potlucks, Thanksgiving, and holiday meals.
Prep Time: 10 minutes minutes
Cook Time: 40 minutes minutes
Cooling Time: 10 minutes minutes
Total Time: 1 hour hour
Servings: 6 servings

Ingredients 

  • 2 large zucchini, cut into ½-inch thick slices OR spiralized
  • 2 large yellow squash, cut into ½-inch thick slices OR spiralized
  • 1 stick (8 tablespoons) unsalted butter, at room temperature
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 3½ cups milk
  • 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1½ tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese OR shredded Parmesan cheese, divided
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese, divided
  • 1 cup panko crumbs
  • olive oil, for drizzling
  • chopped fresh parsley, for garnish
  • grated Parmesan cheese, for garnish, optional

Instructions 

  • Preheat: Preheat the oven to 375˚F.
  • Prep: Grease a 9×13-inch baking dish with a little butter and set aside. Cut the zucchini and yellow squash into ½-inch thick slices. Place in a colander, sprinkle with salt, and let drain.
  • Make the cream sauce: Melt the butter in a 2-quart saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 2 minutes, continuing to whisk. Slowly add the milk and Italian seasoning, whisking constantly until smooth. Simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, whisking frequently, until the sauce thickens.
  • Season the vegetables: Use paper towels to pat the squash and zucchini as dry as possible, then transfer them to a large bowl. Add the garlic, ½ tablespoon of olive oil, and salt and pepper to taste. Toss to combine.
  • Sauté: Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the squash and zucchini and cook for 3 minutes, or until they just begin to soften, stirring frequently. Remove from the heat, drain any excess juices, and set aside.
  • Layer: Spoon ¾ cup of the cream sauce over the bottom of the prepared baking dish. Arrange half of the squash mixture over the sauce. Sprinkle with 1 cup of mozzarella and ⅓ cup of Parmesan. Add the remaining squash mixture, then cover with the remaining cream sauce and sprinkle with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan. Sprinkle the Panko breadcrumbs over the top and drizzle with a little olive oil.
  • Bake: Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the casserole is bubbling around the edges.
  • Cool, then serve: Remove from the oven and let cool for 10 minutes. Top the casserole with fresh parsley and grated Parmesan. Serve warm.

How to Store and Reheat Leftovers

  • Refrigerate: Cover the leftovers tightly or transfer them to an airtight container and refrigerate for up to 4 days.
  • Freeze: Transfer the cooled casserole to freezer-safe containers or freezer bags, removing as much air as possible before sealing. Freeze for up to 2 months for the best quality.
  • Reheat: Place the desired portion in an oven-safe dish and bake at 350˚F until heated through. Frozen leftovers will take longer to reheat, so make sure the casserole is hot all the way through before serving.
