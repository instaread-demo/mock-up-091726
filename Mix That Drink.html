19 Frangelico Drinks You Need to Try

Sometimes you just want cozy, nutty, coffee-and-cream vibes. This list rounds up the best Frangelico cocktails for you to try. Think hazelnut-forward, chocolate, caramel, espresso and a few fun dessert twists.

These are easy crowd-pleasers you can shake, stir, or blend, whether you want something light and sweet or full-on milkshake mode.

Here’s what to expect:

  • Smooth after-dinner sips with cream, coffee, and hazelnut notes
  • Dessert-y treats that taste like candy bars (Snickers, M&M’s, s’mores—yep)
  • Simple mixed drinks and layered show-offs
  • Helpful tips for balancing sweetness and getting that perfect texture

Grab some ice, your favorite liqueurs, and a shaker. Let’s make cozy, nutty magic in a glass.

Frangelico Drinks

Road Streaker Cocktail Recipe

Creamy coffee meets peach and hazelnut in the Road Streaker cocktail, balanced by vodka and heavy cream for a smooth, not-too-sweet sip perfect after dinner.

Satin Angel Drink Recipe

The Satin Angel drink recipe blends cream with Frangelico and a dash of cola over ice. It's sweet and unique, and very light on the alcohol.

Crispy Crunch Cocktail Recipe

The Crispy Crunch cocktail is named for a chocolate and peanut bar made by Cadbury. It features Kahlua, Creme de Cacao and Frangelico and Metaxa brandy.

Nutty Irishman Cocktail Recipe

The Nutty Irishman cocktail combines Irish whiskey, Baileys, and Frangelico for a sweet, nutty delight perfect for dessert-like sipping.

Hazelnut Espresso Martini

A creamy Hazelnut Espresso Martini with Frangelico, fresh espresso, and vodka makes an easy, crowd-pleasing coffee cocktail for any gathering.

Caramel Martini

The Caramel Martini is definitely a taste experience. Like most candy-tinis, it's all alcohol. But there's not a hint of gin or vodka anywhere near this recipe. You get hazelnut, an almost buttery vanilla flavor and butterscotch, all mixed up into a wonderful caramel flavor.

Liquid Snickers Drink Recipe

If the idea of turning a Snickers bar into alcoholic liquid repulses you, stop reading immediately. The Liquid Snickers cocktail is just what it sounds like.

Cafe Nelson Drink Recipe

A cozy Cafe Nelson blends Baileys, Frangelico, and hot coffee with a soft whipped cream float, offering nutty warmth, gentle sweetness, and easy serving tips.

Caramel "J" Cocktail Recipe

The Caramel "J" is one of those drink recipes you might refer to as an alcoholic milkshake. The addition of a ripe banana makes it especially smooth and rich. It's definitely dessert in a glass.

Smooth Operator Drink Recipe

Hazelnut and coffee meet Irish cream and banana in a rich, chilled cocktail with plush mouthfeel, easy shaking tips, and smart brand picks for balance.

Frangelico Float Drink Recipe

A chocolate-hazelnut milkshake cocktail pairs Frangelico and creme de cacao with vanilla ice cream, finished with whipped cream and grated hazelnuts.

M&M Cocktail Recipe

A candy-inspired sip that tastes like an M&M, made with Frangelico and creme de cacao. Shaken over ice and perfect as a short drink or cocktail.

White Flag Cocktail Recipe

The White Flag is a layered cocktail. Unlike many of them, such as the B52, this one just uses two liqueurs, which makes it much less intimidating to pour.

Reindeer Tracks Martini Drink Recipe

The Reindeer Tracks Martini features chocolate vodka, creme de cacao, Frangelico and cream. It's a rich, creamy, sweet treat of a drink that's hard to stop with just one.

Sandbar Sleeper Shooter Drink

Perfectly balanced flavors of vodka, Baileys, Kahlua, and Frangelico create an irresistible creamy cocktail that shines as a sweet party favorite.

Jamaican Quaalude

The Jamaican Quaalude cocktail offers a delicious mix of coconut, coffee, and nutty liqueurs, making it an irresistible creamy treat.

Palomino Cocktail Recipe

Unusual flavors and a mix of whiskey, Creme de Cassis, and herbal liqueurs make the Palomino a stand-out choice for cocktail fans.

Ichabod Crane Cocktail

The Ichabod Crane is an ice cream based cocktail that tastes like pumpkin spice and rum. It makes a great dessert drink!

Sleigh Bells S'mores Cocktail

Sleigh Bells S'mores cocktail blends s'mores vodka, Frangelico, and ginger beer for a toasty, nutty drink, perfect for cozy evenings by the fireplace.
